## Level 6 Gate — Fixed Inputs (locked, not redefined here)

- **World:** manual technical drafting, drawing offices, 1920s–1970s. Rejects ornamentation, trend, digital-perfect slickness. Central figures present by **choice** (mastery, apprenticeship) — full aspirational identity transfer is legitimate.
- **Identity Transfer:** *"I am calibrated. My judgment is exact, not decorated."*
- **Symbolic Density checklist:** name (Datum), silhouette (compass/protractor/T-square derived), materials (titanium/brushed steel/technical acetate), color logic (graphite, vellum cream, blueprint navy, brass/steel), hardware (fine-adjustment-screw hinge, knurled grip), voice (technical annotation), ownership ritual (spec card), packaging (instrument roll).
- **Binding condition carried forward:** none of the above may be marketed as already real until it's physically built. This governs every Level 8 and Level 11 claim below.

No contradiction found. Gate clears.

## Escalation Check

No node failure traces back to a flaw in the World, Identity Transfer, or Symbolic Density. Every Conditional Pass in this document traces to the **same single disclosed dependency**: physical execution (hinge manufacturing, spec-card system, real production numbers, real insider validators) hasn't happened yet. **No escalation back to brand-forge is required.** This is the architecture correctly refusing to paper over an unbuilt product with persuasion technique — exactly the failure mode this skill exists to prevent.

## Status Summary

- **Launch-ready as written:** message architecture, persuasion mechanics structure, channel rules, distribution logic, conversion resolution for fit-trust, retention touchpoint design.
- **Not launch-ready until built:** the hinge mechanism, the spec-card system, real production numbers, and at least one real third-party/insider validator. Until these exist, do not publish any Level 8 claim as fact — hold that copy in draft.
- **Single point of failure to watch:** Level 12.3. If the in-hand product doesn't deliver on the promise, the whole closing-to-retention chain fails retroactively, not just Level 12.

**This document does not authorize shipping proof claims (hinge specifics, production numbers, third-party validation) that aren't true yet.** Everything else — voice, channel rules, message architecture, objection handling for what's already real — is ready to use now.
